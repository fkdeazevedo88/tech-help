Your safety

How to tell real tech help from a scam

Fake "tech support" calls are common. Here is how we work, so you can always tell us apart.

What we do

  • Only help when you contact us first.
  • Tell you the price before we start.
  • Let you watch everything and stop us anytime.
  • Disconnect when we're done, and show you how to check.

What we never do

  • Call you out of the blue about a "virus".
  • Ask for gift cards, crypto or your bank password.
  • Ask you to log in to your bank while we're connected.
  • Rush you or threaten that your computer will be "locked".

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to install anything?

Only a free, well-known screen-sharing app that we walk you through on the phone. You can remove it afterwards, and we'll show you how.

Can you see my files after we're done?

No. The connection only works while the window is open and you've clicked "Allow". Once you close it, we can't connect again without a new code from you.

What if you can't fix it?

Then you don't pay. If it's a hardware problem, like a broken screen, we'll tell you honestly and suggest a local repair shop.

How do I pay?

Interac e-Transfer or credit card, after the problem is fixed. We email you a receipt.

Can you help my parents who live far away?

Yes. You can book and pay for them, and we'll call them directly at a time that suits them.
