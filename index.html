Safety first

When we'll tell you to stop and call a pro

Some jobs are dangerous or, by law, need a licensed technician. We'll always say so, even if it means a shorter call.

Not DIY: we'll send you to a licensed technician

  • Anything on gas lines, gas valves or burners
  • Adding or handling refrigerant (AC, heat pumps, freezers)
  • Work inside the electrical panel, or new wiring that needs a permit
  • Sparks, burning smells, or melted wires and plugs

Good DIY, with us guiding you

  • Filters, drains, resets and cleaning
  • Finding the problem so you buy the right part
  • Swapping simple parts with the power or water off
  • Deciding if a repair is worth it, or getting a second opinion on a quote

Smell gas? Leave the building now, then call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Don't call us first.

Questions

Good to know

I'm not handy at all. Can I still do this?

Most calls are simple: a reset, a filter, a clogged drain or a tripped breaker. We go one small step at a time, and we'll tell you if a job is beyond a first-timer.

What do I need for the call?

A phone with a camera, and the model sticker of the equipment if you can find it. For some jobs, basic tools like a screwdriver or a flashlight. We tell you before we start.

Do you help businesses?

Yes. Restaurants, stores and landlords call us about walk-in coolers, ice machines, rooftop units and more, often to decide whether it's an emergency before paying for an after-hours visit.

What if it can't be fixed by video?

We tell you what's wrong as best we can, so the technician you call knows what to bring, and you're harder to overcharge.

How do I pay?

After the call, by Interac e-Transfer or card. We email you a receipt.
